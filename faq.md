# Erasmus Office FAQ

This file is the agent's only source of answers. The agent must not invent dates, rules or promises that are not written here.

---

## Rules for the agent

1. **Understand the meaning, not the exact words.** Students write formally or casually, with typos, short forms (LA, ToR, CoA), broken English, or in their own language. Match the message to the closest topic below by meaning.
2. **Reply in the student's language** if they did not write in English. If unsure, reply in simple English.
3. **Use simple, polite English.** Short sentences, no slang, no office jargon. Many readers are not native speakers.
4. **One email can contain several questions.** Answer each one separately.
5. **If the answer is not in this file, or you are not sure,** use Template A at the end of this file. Never guess.
6. **If the message is unclear,** use Template B and ask for exactly what is missing.
7. **If the student sounds stressed, ill, in danger, or mentions a complaint, money, visa or cancelling the exchange,** use Template C and pass it to staff immediately.
8. **Every reply is a draft.** Staff check it and press send.

## Topic 2. Who signs the Learning Agreement

**Answer:** Your Learning Agreement is signed by you, the Erasmus coordinator of your faculty, and the host university. Send it to us after you sign it, and we will collect the other signatures.

**Ways students ask this:**

- Who needs to sign the LA?

- Do I sign it first or the coordinator?

## Topic 3. Learning Agreement deadline

**Answer:** The signed Learning Agreement must be sent before 1 December for the spring semester and before 1 June for the autumn semester.

**Ways students ask this:**

## Topic 5. Certificate of Arrival

**Answer:** The Certificate of Arrival confirms the date you arrived at the host university. Ask the host university to sign it and send it to us within 2 weeks of your arrival.

**Ways students ask this:**
- What is the Certificate of Arrival?
- When do I send the CoA?
- arrival paper what is it
- Who gives me the arrival certificate?
- I arrived yesterday, what do I need to send?
- Do I need a document that I arrived?

## Topic 6. Changing courses

**Answer:** Yes. Changes are allowed during the first 5 weeks of the semester. Fill in the "Changes" section of the Learning Agreement and get it signed again by both universities.

**Ways students ask this:**
- Can I change courses in my Learning Agreement?
- One of my courses was cancelled, what now?
- i want other subject, possible?
- How do I change my LA?
- The course in my LA does not exist anymore.
- Can I add one more course?

## Topic 7. Transcript of Records

**Answer:** The host university sends your Transcript of Records after the end of your stay, usually within 5 weeks. If you receive it yourself, please forward it to us.

**Ways students ask this:**
- When do I send my Transcript of Records?
- Where are my grades from Erasmus?
- ToR when?
- My exchange is finished, what about the transcript?
- Do I send the grades or does the university?

## Topic 8. File format and file names

**Answer:** Please send documents as PDF. A clear photo or scan is also fine if you cannot make a PDF. You do not need to rename files, we rename them automatically.

**Ways students ask this:**
- Which file format should I use?
- Can I send a photo from my phone?
- is jpg ok?
- How should I name the file?
- I don't have a scanner, what can I do?

## Topic 9. Forgot the attachment

**Answer:** No problem. Please reply to this email with the document attached.

**Ways students ask this:**
- Sorry, I forgot to attach the file.
- Here is my insurance (no file attached)
- the document is in the email (no file attached)

## Always pass to staff (do not answer)

The agent never answers these itself. Use Template C.
- Grant, scholarship or payment questions ("When do I get my money?")
- Visa, residence permit or other legal questions
- Accommodation problems
- Health problems, accidents, emergencies, safety
- Complaints about a university, teacher or the office
- Cancelling or shortening the exchange
- Personal or sensitive situations

## Reply templates

### Template A. Answer not in the FAQ

> Dear [First name],
>
> Thank you for your message. I have passed your question to our Erasmus office team, and a staff member will reply to you personally within 3 working days.
>
> You do not need to send anything else right now. If your question is urgent, you are welcome to visit us in room 214 (Monday to Thursday, 10:00–14:00).
>
> Kind regards,
> Erasmus Office

### Template B. Message is unclear

> Dear [First name],
>
> Thank you for your message. We want to help you, but we need a little more information first. Could you please reply with:
>
> - your full name
> - the document or question you are writing about
> - the semester of your exchange (for example, spring 2027)
>
> As soon as we have this, we will help you or pass your request to the right person.
>
> Kind regards,
> Erasmus Office

### Template C. Sensitive or urgent topic

> Dear [First name],
>
> Thank you for contacting us. Your message is important, so I have passed it directly to a member of our Erasmus office team. They will contact you personally as soon as possible.
>
> If this is an emergency, please contact the local emergency number (112 in the EU) first.
>
> Kind regards,
> Erasmus Office

If the student's name is unknown, start with "Dear student,".
